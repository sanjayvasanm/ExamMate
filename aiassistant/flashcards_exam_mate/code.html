QUESTION

What is the derivative of sin(x)?

touch_app Click to flip & reveal answer
Answer & Explanation Verified Fact

SOLUTION

d/dx [sin(x)] = cos(x)

By fundamental trigonometric differentiation, the rate of change of the sine function at any real value x is given by the cosine function.
